# Garden texture credits

`foliage.webp` (1024 px, desktop) and `foliage-512.webp` (phones) form one alpha-tested atlas used by
the photo vegetation of every garden (engine/garden/photo.js). They were composed once, offline,
from these **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** Poly Haven
assets (2k PNG / 1k JPG maps, recomposed into leaf clusters, shrub, flower and grass cards and a bark strip).
Attribution is not required by the licence; it is given here as thanks.

| Use in the atlas | Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| tree leaf clusters, bark | Island Tree 02 (`island_tree_02`: leaves_diff, leaves_alpha, branches_diff) | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02) | CC0 1.0 |
| shrub / olive leaves | Shrub 04 (`shrub_04`: diff, alpha) | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04) | CC0 1.0 |
| flowering shrub / climbers | Periwinkle Plant (`periwinkle_plant`: diff, opacity) | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/periwinkle_plant) | CC0 1.0 |
| grass tufts | Grass Medium 01 (`grass_medium_01`: diff, alpha) | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01) | CC0 1.0 |

Authors (Poly Haven): Island Tree 02 — Rob Tuytel, Rico Cilliers; Shrub 04 — Rico Cilliers;
Periwinkle Plant — Amal Kumar; Grass Medium 01 — Rob Tuytel, Rico Cilliers.

## Environment HDRI (`public/env/`)

**Sunny Rose Garden** (`sunny_rose_garden`, 2k .hdr) by Dimitrios Savva (photography) and Jarod Guest
(processing), [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/sunny_rose_garden), CC0 1.0. The sun is removed and
the sky re-encoded as WebP (1024 × 512 desktop, 256 × 128 phones)
Used for reflections and as the sky of the lightmap bake.
