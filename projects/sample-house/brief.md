# Brief — Sample House / Rumah Contoh

> A fictional family house used to demonstrate the engine. Rumah fiktif untuk contoh.

## Household / Penghuni
- 2 adults + 2 children; guests stay over now and then. / 2 dewasa + 2 anak; sesekali ada tamu menginap.
- Work from home sometimes (corner in the family lounge). / Kadang kerja dari rumah (sudut di ruang santai).

## Plot / Tanah
- 19 × 30 m (570 m²), flat, road on the south side. / Datar, jalan di sisi selatan.
- City: Bandung (≈ 6.9° S), tropical highland climate, 18–29 °C, ±2,200 mm rain per year.

## Style / Gaya
- Tropical modern: warm white render, charcoal tile hip roof with deep 0.9 m eaves, black steel window frames,
  oak & teak, sage and cream fabrics, plenty of plants. / Tropis modern.
- Cross ventilation: windows on two sides of most rooms. / Ventilasi silang.

## Budget & priorities / Anggaran & prioritas

- Rooftop solar on the north, east and west roof planes.
